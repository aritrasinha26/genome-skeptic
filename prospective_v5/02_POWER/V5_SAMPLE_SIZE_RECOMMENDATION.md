# V5 sample-size recommendation

M60 had zero Agentic-versus-Deterministic endpoint discordances. The
scenarios below are design assumptions, not estimates.

## Predeclared alternatives

CONSERVATIVE (q=0.20, p=0.70) is the scientifically cautious alternative
because no positive discordance rate is empirically established.

That alternative does **not** reach 80% power at any tested N (40–120).
At N=120, conservative power is 0.423.

The smallest tested N that reaches >=80% power under a named scenario
other than an unpowered conservative assumption is therefore used:

**Recommended N = 120** under the **MODERATE** alternative
(q=0.30, p=0.75; expected 36 discordant pairs; power=0.833).

## Minimum N among tested values with power >= 0.80

| scenario | q | p | min N | expected discordant pairs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CONSERVATIVE | 0.20 | 0.70 | none among 40–120 | n/a |
| MODERATE | 0.30 | 0.75 | 120 | 36 |
| STRONG | 0.40 | 0.80 | 60 | 24 |

## Power at selected N

| N | conservative q=0.20 p=0.70 | moderate q=0.30 p=0.75 | strong q=0.40 p=0.80 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40 | 0.1080 | 0.3074 | 0.6219 |
| 60 | 0.1918 | 0.4954 | 0.8221 |
| 80 | 0.2776 | 0.6342 | 0.9250 |
| 100 | 0.3535 | 0.7466 | 0.9722 |
| 120 | 0.4227 | 0.8334 | 0.9902 |

N=40 is **not** powered merely because it contains 40 cases. It does not
reach 80% power under CONSERVATIVE, MODERATE, or STRONG.

N=60 is the smallest tested N powered only under STRONG.

## Recommended prospective cohort

N = 120 evaluable genomes

60 independently established tet(A)/tet(B) POSITIVE

60 independently established tet(A)/tet(B) NEGATIVE

This is a deliberately balanced validation cohort, not a prevalence
sample. Truth must not be derived from Genome Skeptic, AMRFinderPlus,
the specialist comparator, or the V5 validator.

The Decision-Authority arm is a prespecified secondary comparison and
was not used for this sample-size calculation.
